=== APP NAME ===
PDF Decryptor - PDDF

=== SHORT DESCRIPTION ===
Easily batch decrypt password-protected PDF files locally on your device.

=== FULL DESCRIPTION ===
PDF Decryptor (PDDF) is a fast, lightweight, and privacy-first tool designed to batch unlock and decrypt password-protected PDF documents on Android.

Whether you need to remove passwords from a single bank statement or process multiple encrypted PDF documents at once, PDF Decryptor handles everything 100% locally on your device. Your sensitive files and passwords never leave your phone.

Key Features:
• Batch PDF Decryption: Select and unlock multiple password-protected PDFs at once with fast, asynchronous background processing.
• Saved Password Vault: Securely store frequently used passwords in an encrypted local database for one-tap autofill.
• Quick Settings Tile & Direct Share: Open PDFs directly from file managers, email attachments, or messaging apps, or trigger decryption instantly from the Quick Settings tile.
• Flexible Output Options: Choose between saving decrypted files as a new copy or overwriting the original file.
• Modern Material Design: Clean Jetpack Compose UI with full Dark Theme and multi-language support.
• 100% Offline & Private: No internet permission requested, zero telemetry, zero ads, and zero data collection.
